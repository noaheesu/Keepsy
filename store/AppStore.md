## English

- **Name:** Keepsy
- **Subtitle:** Home stock & expiry countdown
- **Promotional text:** See what runs out first, get one gentle reminder a day, and rebuy the things you always need without typing a thing.
- **Keywords:** fridge,pantry,expiration,expiry,grocery,shopping list,food waste,inventory,best before,freezer
- **Description:**

Is there milk at home? Did the yogurt go off? Keepsy keeps track so you don't have to.

Count down to every date
Each item shows how many days it has left, and the list puts what runs out first at the top: past date, today or tomorrow, this week, later.

Add things in seconds
Point the camera at the package to read the date. Things you've bought before fill in their place and shelf life by themselves.

Keep up with a swipe
Swipe left when you finish something, right when you buy it again. Touch and hold to set how much is left, move it to the freezer, or mark it as thrown away.

Shopping that remembers
Items that run out show up as suggestions, and so do things you usually buy around now. Check them off at the store and put them all away with one tap.

Every room, not just the fridge
Make your own places, like Kitchen › Fridge › Top shelf or Bathroom › Cabinet, and see what's in each one.

One reminder a day
Pick a time and get a single notification with what expires soon.

Private by design
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your items stay in your own iCloud.

모든 날짜를 디데이로
물건마다 남은 날을 보여주고, 기한 지남 · 오늘·내일 · 이번 주 · 넉넉해요 순으로 먼저 써야 할 걸 위에 올려요.

몇 초 만에 등록
포장지에 카메라를 비추면 날짜를 읽어요. 전에 산 물건은 장소와 기한이 알아서 채워져요.

밀어서 관리
다 쓰면 왼쪽, 또 사면 오른쪽으로 밀어요. 길게 누르면 남은 양을 바꾸거나 냉동실로 옮기거나 버렸다고 표시할 수 있어요.

기억하는 장보기
다 떨어진 물건, 평소 살 때가 된 물건을 추천해요. 마트에서 체크하고 "집에 넣기" 한 번이면 끝.

냉장고만이 아니라 집 전체
부엌 › 냉장고 › 윗칸, 욕실 › 수납장처럼 장소를 직접 만들고 장소별로 확인해요.

알림은 하루 한 번
원하는 시간에 곧 기한이 끝나는 것만 모아서 알려드려요.

개인정보는 그대로
계정, 광고, 추적 없음. 기록은 내 iCloud에만 있어요.
